Our story

From a foster network to a full shelter

Paws & Care Animal Shelter was founded in 2014 by a small group of animal lovers in Pretoria who wanted to help stray and abandoned dogs and cats.

Our history

What began as a foster network run from volunteers' homes grew into a registered non-profit organisation with its own shelter building in 2020. Paws & Care now rescues, cares for, and rehomes over 300 animals every year — but still relies heavily on word of mouth and social media.

2014

A foster network is born

A handful of volunteers begin fostering strays out of their own homes across Pretoria.

2020

Our own shelter building

Paws & Care registers as a non-profit and moves into a dedicated shelter facility.

Today

300+ animals a year

We rescue, rehabilitate and rehome hundreds of dogs and cats annually, community-funded and community-run.

Mission

To rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome abandoned and abused animals, and to promote responsible pet ownership in our community.

Vision

A community where every dog and cat has a safe, loving home, and animal cruelty no longer exists.

Who we serve

Animal lovers wanting to adopt a pet, local residents wanting to report strays, donors and sponsors, and volunteers who want to help at the shelter.

Where the animals stay

Our facility

A modest, well-loved shelter building in Pretoria, with room to rest, room to play, and room to heal.

Kennels & cattery

Clean, climate-controlled spaces for every dog and cat in our care.

Intake & vet room

Every new arrival gets a health check, vaccinations, and any urgent treatment.

Outdoor exercise yard

Space to stretch legs, play, and socialise before finding a permanent home.
